# 🔐 Security Policy

At **OverLab Group**, we take security seriously.  
We appreciate the efforts of security researchers and the community to help keep our projects safe.

## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability in any OverLab project, **please do NOT open a public issue**.

Instead, report it privately via email:

📧 **security@overlab.group** (if available)  
📧 otherwise: **overlab.group@gmail.com** with the subject: `[SECURITY] Project Name`

We aim to respond within **48 hours** and will work with you to understand and resolve the issue promptly.

## 🔄 Responsible Disclosure

We kindly ask that you:
- Allow us reasonable time to investigate and address the issue before public disclosure.
- Provide sufficient details to reproduce the issue.
- Act in good faith to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, or service disruption.

## 🏆 Recognition

We believe in giving credit where it’s due.  
With your permission, we’ll thank you in the release notes once the issue is resolved.

---

> OverLab is built by the community, for the community.  
> Your help in keeping it secure means the world to us. 🖤
