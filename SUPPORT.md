# 🆘 Getting Support for OverLab Projects

We’re glad you’re here. Whether you’re stuck, curious, or just want to chat — there are several ways to get help.

---

## 📚 Documentation First

Before reaching out, please check:
- The project’s **README.md**
- Any **docs/** folder in the repository
- Opened and closed **Issues** (your question may already be answered)

---

## 💬 GitHub Discussions

Most questions, ideas, and general conversations belong here.

👉 **[Join the Discussion](https://github.com/orgs/OverLab-Group/discussions)**

This is the best place for:
- “How do I…?”
- “What’s the best way to…?”
- Feature brainstorming
- Showing off what you built with OverLab

---

## 🐛 Bug Reports & Feature Requests

If you’ve found a bug or have a concrete idea for a new feature, please **open an issue** in the relevant repository.

Use the **issue templates** — they help us understand your request faster.

---

## 📧 Security Reports

**Never** report security vulnerabilities in public issues.  
Instead, follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

---

## 📬 Direct Contact

For **private** matters (not support questions), you can reach the core team at:

📧 **overlab.group@gmail.com**

Please use this only for:
- Code of Conduct reports
- RFC submissions (see CONTRIBUTING.md)
- Legal or partnership inquiries

---

## 🖤 Community‑Driven Support

OverLab is maintained by volunteers. We do our best to respond quickly, but please be patient and respectful.

**Help us help you:**
- Be specific about your problem
- Include your environment (OS, runtime version, etc.)
- Share error messages or logs

---

> Together, we build the future. 🚀
